Privacy Policy for M Music

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the M Music mobile application (“the app,” “we,” or “our”) collects and processes information. M Music is developed and published by One2 Labs.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or the contents of your media files.

However, the app uses Google Firebase services to help improve quality, performance, and stability.

a) Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect usage and technical information, such as:

  • App interactions, including screens and features used
  • Device information, such as model and operating system version
  • Language and general country or region information
  • App version and installation-related information
  • General performance and technical data
  • IP address, as processed by Google according to its policies

b) Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports when the app stops working unexpectedly. Crash reports may include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Technical crash details, such as stack traces
  • General country or region information

We do not directly collect:

  • Your name or contact details
  • Your photos, videos, or audio files
  • Your contacts
  • Your precise GPS location
  • Sensitive personal data

Analytics and crash data are processed according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

We use analytics and crash data for the following purposes:

  • Improving app performance and stability
  • Detecting and fixing technical issues
  • Understanding feature usage
  • Enhancing the user experience

We do not sell personal information or share information with advertisers for advertising purposes.

3. Local and Network Media Playback

M Music allows you to play media stored on your device and may support media streaming over supported network protocols, such as HTTP, HTTPS, SMB, or DLNA, depending on the app's functionality.

Your local media files remain on your device and are not uploaded to or transmitted to us.

If you choose to save login credentials for network sources, they are stored locally on your device and are not shared with us.

The app does not use cookies for its local media playback features.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

Google Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

M Music is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review the concern.

6. Data Security

We do not operate servers that store user records. Analytics and crash information are processed and stored by Google Firebase, which applies its own security measures.

7. Data Retention and User Control

Google Firebase retains analytics and crash data according to its applicable policies and retention settings.

Depending on your device and Google account settings, you may be able to manage data collection by:

  • Resetting or deleting your device's Advertising ID
  • Managing analytics and advertising settings in Google settings
  • Uninstalling the app

For questions or concerns regarding data, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
